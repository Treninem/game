# ImPuls — CC0 3D Model Expansion (2026-08-14)

Only sources verified as CC0/public-domain are approved here. Do not collect assets merely because they exist: every pack must solve a world, gameplay, UI, animation, ambience or optimization need. Prefer GLB/glTF for Godot, retain source/license metadata, and optimize before runtime use.

- Growing crop stages and harvested variants.

- Icons/thumbnails for inventory, crafting, quests, map, status effects and encyclopedia.
- Portraits/silhouettes for NPCs/animals.
- Placeholder assets must be explicitly tagged so they cannot silently ship as final art.

## Selection policy — quality over hoarding
1. Search broadly, but import narrowly: a pack must fill a real category or improve quality/coverage.
2. Prefer a small number of coherent families over hundreds of visually incompatible one-offs.
3. Exact license verification is mandatory; CC0 preferred. Reject unknown, NC, ND, editorial-only, ripped or branded-game assets.
4. Prefer creator/official download pages.
5. Every third-party directory requires `SOURCE.md` and `LICENSE.txt`.
6. Prefer GLB/glTF; preserve source only when needed for editing/rigging.
7. Normalize Godot scale, axes, materials, skeleton/animation naming, collisions and pivots.
8. Generate LODs and collision proxies; compress/atlas textures where useful.
9. Deduplicate meshes/materials/textures and do not commit giant raw archives blindly.
10. Maintain coherent ImPuls art direction by rematerialing/recoloring compatible CC0 assets.
11. Asset registry must distinguish VERIFIED, CANDIDATE, PLACEHOLDER and PRODUCTION-READY.
12. Before adding another asset, ask: what gameplay/world problem does this solve, and is there already a better asset in the repository?
