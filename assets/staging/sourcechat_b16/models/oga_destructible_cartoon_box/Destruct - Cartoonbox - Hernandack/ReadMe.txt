Low Poly Destructible Cartoon Box - 4 variants
Created in Blender 2.8x
By Hernandack (https://hernandack.com / https://hernandack.itch.io)

4 variants of cartoon boxes with stripes (material)
Format base obj, destructible glb/gltf, and mtl.
All desctrutibles has -chunk postfix on it's name.

CC-0
Free for personal and commercial use

For the materials basically just 3, Base, Dark stripe and white stripe, use accordingly.
Just use the chunks as RigidBody 3D then the physics will do it's magic. Tested in Godot Engine 4-beta17
