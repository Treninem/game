Animation sources: existing CC0 Quaternius Animated Human, B36 RPG Ranger bow clips, Universal Animation Library 2 Standard TreeChopping. They are reused from the existing citizen.glb rather than downloaded again. Reproduction: tools/build_realistic_humans.py. Current garments are a recolored generic shirt/trousers and plain leather shoes, not a complete armor wardrobe. No photorealistic or Witcher 3 quality claim.

Garment refinement: authored UV islands determine shirt/trouser material assignment, cuffs are fitted to native wrists, and connected-topology weights are smoothed for deformation. Body faces masked by the authored garment/shoe masks are removed. Knee IK selects the forward bend solution while preserving the animated ankle position. Gait retargeting excludes the asymmetric combat Idle offset. The actual footwear establishes floor contact for grounded clips; knee IK preserves ankle position. Torso lean and knee direction are sampled by the geometry validator.
