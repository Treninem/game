# ImPuls capital canal kit

Original ImPuls Blender bridge and quay geometry, CC0 1.0. No new third-party
download or concept-image pixels. Editable source:
`assets/source/architecture/capital_canal/CapitalCanalKit.blend`.
Canonical interchange: `assets/production/architecture/capital_canal/CapitalCanalKit.glb`.
The blend retains connected photographic materials and relative image links.
Export in Blender with `tools/export_capital_canal_kit.py`, selecting a NEW
private output directory in `IMPULS_CANAL_TREE`; the source is never overwritten.

Five mesh parts, 43,348 triangles: deck/approaches 1,408; three arch vaults
14,784; piers/spandrels 13,504; parapets/caps 10,736; six-metre ashlar quay
module 2,916. The four physical bridges share exact mesh collision with open
vaults, a 96 x 16 m footprint and a 14 m clear lane. Quay modules leave gaps at
bridge crossings. No full collision box seals the canal under a bridge.

Existing unmodified CC0 OGA arikel/CruzR diffuse photos `stone_paving.jpg` and
`lichen_rock.jpg` are referenced from `assets/production/materials/ground/`.
Their SOURCE.md/LICENSE.txt remain authoritative. The original ImPuls fine
stone normal/ORM files are shared from `assets/production/architecture/city_street/`.
No full scanned PBR claim. The runtime's canal ashlar normal is High/Ultra only.
Existing meadow, temperate trees, household props, stall and lantern assets
retain their separate canonical source/license records.

INTEGRATED in the review branch only after main-integration promotion. This is
one 600 m canal neighbourhood within the existing canonical capital district,
four bridges, two banks, one working two-adult household and a finite shop and
fletcher workshop. Not 20 completed cities, a completed lake/sluice, a full
citizen life simulation, accepted human artwork or final concept quality.
